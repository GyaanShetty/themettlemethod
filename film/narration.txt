Everything left alone… falls apart.
Stone. Empires. Men.
We were given more comfort than any people in history. And we have never been weaker.
Time slips through our fingers.
But two thousand years ago, an emperor wrote by candlelight.
A slave broke his chains, with his mind.
They called it Stoicism. A discipline. Kept daily.
We are the ones who keep it.
Four pillars. Mind. Body. Faith. Brotherhood.
One lesson. Every day. Sealed.
Nine ranks. Earned in days. Never bought.
Open to all. Kept by few.
The flame is lit.
The Mettle Method.
Obey the process.
